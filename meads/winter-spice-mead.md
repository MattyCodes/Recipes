# Winter Spice Mead

Mixed berries and black tea fermented with honey, aged on oak, mulling spices and vanilla.

|             |                                                          |
| ----------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Vessel**  | 1 gal fermenter, 2–3 in headspace                        |
| **Yeast**   | K1-V1116 or similar                                      |
| **Primary** | 3–4 weeks                                                |
| **Aged**    | 6 months                                                 |
| **OG → FG** | 1.102 → 1.000, 13.4% ABV (author's batch)                |
| **Source**  | [Golden Hive Mead — Starter Kit Guide](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0831/9493/7643/files/Starter_Kit_Guide_6a87fdf8-790c-44a3-9d22-23d8dfaf4220.pdf?v=1727883900), p. 31        |

## Ingredients

**Day of batching**

- 2.5 lbs raw wildflower honey
- K1-V1116 yeast or similar
- Fermaid-O nutrient
- ¾ lbs grapes
- ¾ lbs cranberries
- ¾ lbs cherries
- 1.5 g black tea leaves
- Spring water, to top up

**Post-fermentation**

- Stabilizers
- Honey, to taste
- 10 g oak cubes
- 1 tbsp mulling spices
- Zest from 1 orange
- ½ split vanilla bean

## Steps

1. Sanitize all equipment.
2. Add the honey, fruit, tea leaves and water to the fermenter, leaving 2–3 in of headspace.
3. Pitch the yeast, then add the first dose of nutrient.
4. Stir until evenly combined and record the starting gravity. Keep taking readings through fermentation.
5. Fit the airlock and ferment to completion at room temperature, out of direct sunlight — usually 3–4 weeks.
6. Continue the nutrient schedule through the first three days of fermentation.
7. Remove the fruit and rack as needed once fermentation finishes.
8. Stabilize, then backsweeten with honey to taste.
9. Stir in acid to taste, if using.
10. Add fining agents if needed.
11. Age on the oak, spices, zest and vanilla, tasting weekly, and pull them once the flavor is right.
12. Bottle.

## Notes

- The guide's steps call for acids to taste but don't list any in the ingredients — add your own or skip the step.
- Author's tasting notes: aromatic, seasonal spice, mixed berry, smooth finish.
