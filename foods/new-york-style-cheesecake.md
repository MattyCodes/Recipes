# New York Style Cheesecake

![Dessert](https://img.shields.io/badge/Dessert-7C3AED?style=flat)

Tall, dense water-bath cheesecake on a graham crust, cut with lemon.

|               |                                                                                          |
| ------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Yield**     | 8–10 slices                                                                              |
| **Active**    | 30 min                                                                                   |
| **Bake**      | 1 hr 40 min                                                                              |
| **Chill**     | 8 hrs minimum, up to 2 days                                                              |
| **Equipment** | 9–10 in springform, extra-wide heavy-duty foil, roasting pan                             |
| **Source**    | [Once Upon a Chef](https://www.onceuponachef.com/recipes/new-york-style-cheesecake.html) |

## Ingredients

**Crust**

- 1½ cups graham cracker crumbs (about 12 crackers)
- 5 tbsp unsalted butter, melted
- 2 tbsp sugar
- ⅛ tsp salt

**Filling**

- 32 oz cream cheese (four 8-oz blocks), fully softened
- 2 cups sugar
- 3 tbsp all-purpose flour
- 4 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 tsp lemon zest, packed
- 2 tsp fresh lemon juice
- ¼ tsp salt
- 6 large eggs
- ½ cup sour cream

## Steps

1. Heat the oven to 375°F / 190°C, rack in the lower middle. Wrap the outside of the springform in two sheets of heavy-duty foil, seamless across the bottom and sides; grease the inside.
2. Mix the crust ingredients and press into an even layer on the pan bottom. Bake 10 min, until set, then set aside.
3. Drop the oven to 325°F / 165°C and put a kettle on to boil.
4. Beat the cream cheese, sugar and flour on medium until just smooth, about 1 min. Scrape the bowl.
5. Beat in the vanilla, zest, lemon juice and salt on low, just until combined.
6. On low, add the eggs one at a time, scraping as needed, then the sour cream. Stop as soon as it's uniform — don't overmix.
7. Set the springform in the roasting pan, pour the batter over the crust, then pour boiling water into the roasting pan to 1 in up the springform's sides.
8. Bake 1½–1¾ hrs, until the center only wobbles slightly when nudged. Tent loosely with foil if the top browns too fast.
9. Cool in the water bath on a wire rack until the water is just warm, about 45 min.
10. Lift the springform out, discard the foil, and run a thin knife around the edge if the cake sticks. Cover and refrigerate at least 8 hrs.
11. Remove the pan sides and serve off the base. Slice with a sharp knife, wiping it clean between cuts.

## Notes

- Soften the cream cheese completely; cold cream cheese leaves lumps.
- Standard 12-in foil leaks — use extra-wide heavy-duty.
- Freezes 3 months: firm up uncovered, then wrap tight. Thaw overnight in the fridge.
- Don't swirl fruit into the batter; serve berry sauce alongside instead.
