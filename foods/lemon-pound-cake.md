# Lemon Pound Cake

Creamed butter pound cake with the zest of two lemons, finished with a lemon glaze.

|            |                                                                                |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Bake**   | 50 min at 160°C / 320°F                                                        |
| **Source** | [r/recipes](https://www.reddit.com/r/recipes/comments/fmwxiz/lemon_pound_cake/) |
| **Video**  | [Walkthrough](https://youtu.be/V66MSYcLXCA)                                    |

## Ingredients

**Cake**

- Zest of 2 lemons
- 130 g butter, softened
- 130 g sugar
- 3 medium eggs
- 150 g cake flour
- 4 g baking powder
- 1 g salt
- 30 g milk
- 1 tbsp lemon juice (about ½ lemon)

**Glaze**

- 125 g powdered sugar
- 25 g lemon juice
- Pistachios or lemon zest, to decorate

## Steps

1. Heat the oven to 160°C / 320°F and prepare the mold.
2. Sift the cake flour, salt and baking powder together and set aside.
3. Whisk the softened butter 2 min.
4. Add the lemon zest, then the sugar in two additions, and whisk 6 min more.
5. Mix in the eggs one at a time.
6. Gently mix in half the dry ingredients.
7. Pour in the milk and lemon juice.
8. Add the rest of the dry ingredients and combine.
9. Bake at 160°C / 320°F for 50 min.
10. Whisk the powdered sugar with the lemon juice, glaze the cooled cake, and top with pistachios or zest.

## Notes

- The post gives no pan size or yield — scale to the mold you have.
