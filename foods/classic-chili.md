# Classic Chili

One-pot beef chili built on toasted spices, beer and a little cocoa powder.

|            |                                                                                            |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Active** | ~25 min                                                                                    |
| **Simmer** | 1 hr or more, uncovered                                                                    |
| **Source** | [Here's Your Bite on TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/@heresyourbite/video/7467751615940873515) |

## Ingredients

**Chili**

- 2–3 tbsp olive oil
- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 1 large jalapeño, diced (seeds removed for less heat)
- 2 tbsp chili powder
- 1 tbsp chipotle chili powder (optional — see notes)
- 2 tbsp cumin
- 1 tbsp smoked paprika
- ½ tbsp oregano
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tbsp sugar
- ½ tbsp black pepper
- ½ tbsp unsweetened cocoa powder
- 1–2 tsp salt, to taste
- 1 tsp mustard powder
- A few dashes of cayenne
- 1 lb lean ground beef
- 4.5 oz tomato paste
- 6–8 garlic cloves, minced
- 12 oz beer (lager, amber or stout)
- 1 cup beef broth (omit for a thicker chili)
- 8 oz tomato sauce
- 14.5 oz fire-roasted diced tomatoes
- 4 oz diced green chilies, hot or mild
- 1 (15.5 oz) can pinto beans, rinsed and drained
- 1 (15.5 oz) can kidney beans, rinsed and drained
- 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 1 tbsp balsamic vinegar
- 1 bay leaf

**Toppings**

- Shredded cheddar, sour cream, green onions, lime
- Chips or crackers, to dip

## Steps

1. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a large pot over medium. Sauté the onion and jalapeño until softened, 3–5 min.
2. Stir in all the spices, chili powder through cayenne, with another splash of oil. Toast a couple of minutes.
3. Clear a space in the pot, add a little more oil, then the beef. Leave it untouched a couple of minutes to build a crust, then flip, break it up and brown through.
4. Clear a space again and add the tomato paste. Let it caramelize for a minute before stirring it through.
5. Add the garlic and cook 30 seconds.
6. Deglaze with the beer, scraping up the brown bits. Pour in the broth, if using, and stir.
7. Stir in the tomato sauce, diced tomatoes, green chilies, beans, Worcestershire and balsamic.
8. Add the bay leaf and bring to a gentle boil, then drop to low. Simmer uncovered at least 1 hr, stirring occasionally — partially cover if you want it thinner.
9. Taste, adjust the seasoning, and serve with the toppings.

## Notes

- The chipotle chili powder is optional. To skip it, use 3 tbsp regular chili powder in place of both powders.
- Thicker chili: leave out the beef broth. Thinner: partially cover the pot while it simmers.
- The source gives no serving count.
