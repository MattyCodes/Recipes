# Marry Me Chicken Mac

One-pan chicken and elbow macaroni cooked in broth, with sun-dried tomatoes and mozzarella.

|            |                                                                                                  |
| ---------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Total**  | About 30 min                                                                                     |
| **Pan**    | One large skillet with a lid                                                                     |
| **Source** | [Cooking in the Midwest on TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/@cookinginthemidwest/video/7503923077428825375) |

## Ingredients

- 1 lb chicken breast, diced small
- 2 tbsp butter
- 2 tbsp oil
- ½ tsp each salt, pepper, oregano, thyme
- 1 tsp each onion powder, garlic powder
- ¼ tsp red pepper flakes
- 1 tbsp minced garlic
- ⅓ cup sun-dried tomatoes, chopped
- 2½ cups elbow noodles, uncooked
- 4 cups chicken broth
- ½ cup milk
- 2½ cups shredded mozzarella
- Parmesan and dried parsley, to finish

## Steps

1. Heat the butter and oil in a large skillet over medium-high. Add the chicken and season with the salt, pepper, oregano, thyme, onion powder, garlic powder and red pepper flakes. Cook until it's nearly done through.
2. Add the minced garlic and sun-dried tomatoes, then the dry noodles and the broth. Stir together well.
3. Cover, and once it's bubbling, drop the heat to medium-low. Cook 12–15 min, until the pasta is where you want it.
4. Stir in the milk and 2 cups of the mozzarella.
5. Scatter the remaining mozzarella over the top. Leave it covered on the stove until it melts, or broil a couple of minutes.
6. Finish with Parmesan and dried parsley.

## Notes

- The pasta goes in dry and cooks in the broth, so nothing else needs a pot.
- Parmesan is listed without a quantity — it's a finishing scatter.
- Makes enough for leftovers the next day.
