# Challah

![Bread](https://img.shields.io/badge/Bread-38BDF8?style=flat)

Soft, egg-rich braided loaf, glossy from an egg-white wash.

|            |                                                                                  |
| ---------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Yield**  | One 16 in loaf                                                                   |
| **Active** | 20 min                                                                           |
| **Rises**  | ~2 hrs, then 60–90 min                                                           |
| **Bake**   | 25–30 min at 375°F / 190°C                                                       |
| **Total**  | 3 hrs 50 min                                                                     |
| **Source** | [King Arthur Baking](https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/recipes/classic-challah-recipe) |

## Ingredients

**Dough**

- 4 to 4¼ cups (480 to 510 g) unbleached all-purpose flour
- ¾ cup (170 g) lukewarm water
- 6 tbsp (74 g) vegetable oil
- 3 tbsp (63 g) honey
- 2 large eggs
- 1 large egg yolk (reserve the white for the wash)
- 1½ tsp (9 g) table salt
- 4 tsp (12 g) instant yeast

**Topping**

- Reserved egg white, beaten with 1 tbsp cold water
- Poppy or sesame seeds (optional)

## Steps

1. Mix and knead all the dough ingredients — by hand, mixer or bread machine — until soft and smooth. If it's very sticky, work in up to ¼ cup (30 g) more flour.
2. Cover and let rise about 2 hrs, until puffy. It won't necessarily double.
3. Deflate gently and turn out onto a lightly greased surface.
4. Divide for your braid: three strands traditional, four or six for a showier loaf.
5. Roll each piece into a rope about 20 in long. If the dough springs back, cover it and rest 10 min before carrying on.
6. Braid the loaf and move it to a greased or parchment-lined baking sheet.
7. Cover with greased plastic wrap and rise 60–90 min, until very puffy. Near the end, set a rack in the upper third of the oven and heat to 375°F / 190°C.
8. Whisk the reserved egg white with 1 tbsp cold water, brush it over the loaf, and sprinkle generously with seeds.
9. Bake on the upper rack 25–30 min, until deep golden and 190°F / 88°C in the center.
10. Cool on a rack.

## Notes

- Weigh the flour if you can — 480 g. Otherwise spoon it into the cup and sweep the top level.
- Make ahead: braid onto the pan, tent with greased plastic wrap and refrigerate overnight. Next morning let it warm and rise 60 min at room temperature, still covered, then bake as written.
- Dries out after about a day, but it's good toasted, in grilled sandwiches, or as French toast.
- For a Rosh Hashanah spiral, roll one 36 in rope, coil it in a greased 9 in cake pan, rise until puffy, and bake as written.
