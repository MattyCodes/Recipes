# Beer Bread

![Bread](https://img.shields.io/badge/Bread-38BDF8?style=flat)

No-yeast quick bread leavened by beer and baking powder, baked under sliced butter.

|            |                                                                                  |
| ---------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Yield**  | One 9×5 in loaf                                                                  |
| **Active** | 10 min                                                                           |
| **Bake**   | 45–55 min at 375°F / 190°C                                                       |
| **Source** | [Half Baked Harvest](https://www.halfbakedharvest.com/5-ingredient-beer-bread/)  |

## Ingredients

- 3 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 tbsp baking powder
- Pinch of kosher salt
- 1 (12 oz) beer
- ¼ cup honey (or brown sugar, but honey is better)
- 6 tbsp cold salted butter, sliced into 8

## Steps

1. Heat the oven to 375°F / 190°C and grease a 9×5 in loaf pan with butter.
2. Combine the flour, baking powder and salt in a mixing bowl. Pour in the beer, add the honey, and mix until just combined.
3. Spoon the batter into the pan and lay the butter slices over the top.
4. Set the pan on a rimmed baking sheet and bake 45–55 min, until the top is lightly browned.
5. Slice and serve warm.

## Notes

- The rimmed baking sheet isn't optional — the butter melts off the top and will run.
- The author favors a pumpkin beer, and prefers honey over brown sugar here.
- From the author in the comments: the batter holds up to 24 hrs ahead, brought back to room temperature before baking. Store the baked loaf airtight on the counter.
