# Banana Bread

![Bread](https://img.shields.io/badge/Bread-38BDF8?style=flat)

Creamed-butter banana bread, mixed in alternating additions to keep the bottom from going dense.

|            |                                                                                    |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Yield**  | One loaf                                                                           |
| **Pan**    | 8.5 × 4.5 × 2.75 in                                                                |
| **Bake**   | 40–45 min at 350°F / 176°C                                                         |
| **Source** | [Jose el Cook on TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/@jose.elcook/video/7504130554640567583) |

## Ingredients

- 2 ripe bananas
- 220 g all-purpose flour
- 8 g baking powder
- 1 g baking soda
- 2.5 g kosher salt
- Pinch of cinnamon
- 75 g butter, softened
- 65 g sugar
- 65 g brown sugar
- 2 eggs, room temperature

## Steps

1. Mash the bananas, leaving a little texture in them.
2. Combine the flour, baking powder, baking soda, salt and cinnamon in a bowl.
3. Cream the butter with both sugars, beating 2–3 min to work air in.
4. Beat in the eggs one at a time.
5. Add a third of the dry ingredients and half the bananas, mixing on low until just incorporated. Repeat until no flour is left. Don't overmix.
6. Grease the pan well and pour in the batter.
7. Bake at 350°F / 176°C for 40–45 min, until a skewer comes out clean.

## Notes

- Use heavily spotted bananas. The creator's whole point is that bad banana bread is usually underripe bananas, not a bad recipe.
- The alternating additions in step 5 are what stop the dense layer forming at the bottom.
- The creator's loaf needed the full 45 min — check with a skewer rather than the clock.
