# Short Rib Ragu

Short ribs braised three hours in red wine and tomato, shredded into a sauce for pappardelle.

|             |                                                                                              |
| ----------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Braise**  | 3 hrs at 325°F / 165°C, covered                                                              |
| **Serve on**| Pappardelle, with fresh Parmesan                                                             |
| **Source**  | [Zealand on TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/@chefzealand/video/7510716930609761566)           |

## Ingredients

- 1 tbsp olive oil
- ½ cup pancetta, diced
- 3 lbs beef short ribs
- Salt
- 1 large onion, finely diced
- 2 large carrots, finely diced
- 3 stalks celery, finely diced
- 5 cloves garlic
- 2 sprigs rosemary
- 4 sprigs thyme
- 4 sprigs parsley
- 2 fresh bay leaves
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 2 cups red wine
- Balsamic vinegar (see notes)
- 1 (28 oz) can tomato purée
- 3 cups beef stock
- 1 Parmesan rind (optional)
- Pappardelle and fresh Parmesan, to serve

## Steps

1. Finely dice the onion, carrots and celery. Tie the rosemary, thyme, parsley and bay leaves into a bouquet garni so they're easy to fish out later.
2. Season the short ribs generously with salt, then sear them in the olive oil until well browned on all sides. Set aside.
3. In the same pot, render the pancetta down slowly.
4. Add the mirepoix to the rendered fat with a generous pinch of salt. Sweat and caramelize, about 10 min.
5. Add the garlic, cook 1 min, then add the tomato paste and let it cook down a couple of minutes.
6. Deglaze with the red wine and let it reduce away.
7. Stir in the balsamic vinegar and the tomato purée, then add the bouquet garni and the Parmesan rind.
8. Return the short ribs, pour in the beef stock, and bring to a boil.
9. Cover and braise in a 325°F / 165°C oven for 3 hrs, until the meat falls off the bone.
10. Skim off the excess fat. Fish out the bones, the Parmesan rind and the bouquet garni, then shred the meat with two forks.
11. Toss pappardelle with some of the sauce in a pan until well coated, and finish with fresh Parmesan.

## Notes

- The method calls for balsamic vinegar but the posted ingredient list leaves it out, with no quantity given anywhere — add it to taste.
- The Parmesan rind is optional. The creator reckons it takes the dish to the next level.
- Ingredients come from the post's caption; the method is transcribed from the video's narration, so the wording is ours.
- No yield given. Three pounds of short ribs is a generous four servings over pasta.
